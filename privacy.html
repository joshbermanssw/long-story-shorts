Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Long Story Shorts ("we") runs a private uploader application that publishes short video clips to our own YouTube channel, @longstoryshortsclips. The application is used only by us and is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

Our application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data.

What data we access

  • With our own authorisation, the application accesses our YouTube channel to upload videos and to read public statistics (such as view counts) for videos we uploaded.
  • The application does not collect, access or store any data about other YouTube users or viewers.

How we store and share data

The OAuth refresh token for our channel is stored only on our own computer, in the macOS Keychain. Video statistics are stored in a local database on the same computer. We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time via the Google security settings page. Locally stored data is deleted within 30 days of revoking access or on request.
